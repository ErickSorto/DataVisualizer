Introduction

Data visualization is the graphical representation of information and data. With visual elements like charts, graphs, and maps, data visualization tools provide an accessible way to see and understand trends, outliers, and patterns in data.

In the world of big data, data visualization tools and technologies are essential to analyze massive amounts of information and make data-driven decisions. Our culture is visual, including everything from art and advertisements to TV and movies, and our eyes are drawn to colors and patterns. Our interaction with data should reflect this reality.

Our App

Choose a local file from your computer, pick the chart type you need (pie chart, box plot, histogram, scatter plot, or linear plot), and select the rows and columns you want to analyze. The app instantly generates the statistical charts for you — no setup, no uploads, no waiting. Tutorial

How to download (for quick download) and use?

  • click the download link (there might have a warning, please ignore it. If you don't know how to keep the file, you can refer to: handle smartscreen warning)
  • after you download it, double click the software. (Might pop up a cmd terminal, just wait for few seconds, the UI will pop up)
  • click 'browse' button to choose your file and check the file you want to analyse or delete. Then, click the 'delete' or 'next' button to continue
  • choose type of chart you want and click 'next' button to continue (or 'back' button to back to the previous page)
  • choose which row/column you want by filling 'data series' part. (excels mixed with text and numbers may cause error/warning)
  • After you fill in all fields, click 'generate' button to generate your chart. (include first element means whether you want to keep the first row/column i.e. your first row is column name (A, AA, AAA,...). you may not want to include them in your statistic, use this to remove them. (don't worry, the legend will not be affect by this))
